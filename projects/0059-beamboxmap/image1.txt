SetMap – automatically applies box texture mapping aligned to the longest edge of a face-oriented bounding box, so textures follow the main direction of beam-like geometry.
RotMap – rotates the existing box texture mapping of selected objects by 90° to its direction.
ScaleMap – sets a new box texture scale, showing the current scale relative to 1.0 before applying it.
RandomizeMap – randomizes the origin of the texture.

Designed to work primarily with cuboid breps, or complex but directional breps; also works on planar surfaces. Since 0.8.13 it also works on parallelepipeds: find the biggest face, take its longest curve, align a frame to it, then map with the aligned bounding box.

If nothing happens, check Properties > Texture Mapping for the "Click here to fix this automatically" button.
